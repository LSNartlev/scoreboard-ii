How to add custom sounds (as of version 2 beta 1):
Step 1: Put your WAV audio files inside the CustomSounds folder.
Step 2: Rename your audio files, depending on what key you want to assign that sound.
  For example: when assigning a file "coinsound.wav" to key 1, rename the file to "1.wav".
  Note: Custom sound effects are currently fixed to the keys 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, and 0.
  Assigning them in other key bindings, as well as proper custom sound support is under development.
Step 3: Open the Scoreboard II app.
